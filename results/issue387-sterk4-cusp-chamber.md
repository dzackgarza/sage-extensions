# #387: the constructor repair, the walk's candidate rules, and the measured decision at Sterk's cusp 4

**Result: the constructor repair and the walk's candidate rules are landed; the
Sterk_4 row is not green, and the measurement names the defect it is blocked
on: the root filter admits a strictly bigger reflection group than the one
whose chamber the card records, so the walk tiles the card's chamber instead of
computing it.**  The issue stays open with that defect.

Specimen: `T_En = U + U(2) + E8(-2)`, the group `Gamma_{En,2}`, the cusp
`Sterk_4`, card `029J` row `gamma-en-2` 4.3.8 (eleven walls, nine of square
`-4` and two of `-2`).

## 1. The two defects the issue names, and the constructor repair

`cusp_chamber()` at Sterk_4 never returned on main: the `vinal` constructor's
`GetIntegerPoints` enumerates the coordinate box between the negative and the
positive row sums of the inserted matrix to find `W`, the coset representatives
of its row lattice, and at this cusp that box has about `1.3e14` points for a
two-element `W` (measured by #362's probe 13, `.tmp/qc-evidence/issue362-probe13.log`;
`|det inserted| = 2`).  The repair, `_vinal_parallelepiped_points` in
`src/sage_extensions/categories/hyperbolic_lattices.py`, returns the same
points in polynomial time: the Smith normal form `D = U * inserted * V` writes
the row lattice, `a * V^{-1}` for `a` in the box of the diagonal runs once
through every coset, and subtracting the floor of the row-basis coordinates
moves each representative into the half-open parallelepiped.  The constructor
mirror `_FilteredVinAl.__init__` uses it; the engine's behaviour changes only
in the time the constructor takes (0.004 s measured at this cusp,
`.tmp/qc-evidence/issue387-probe-a.log`).

## 2. The decision the issue prescribes, and the measurement

The issue's decision procedure asks whether the roots `FundCone()` returns form
a fundamental system of the finite reflection group generated by the roots
orthogonal to the controlling vector (Vinberg, *Some arithmetical discrete
groups in Lobachevskii spaces*, 1975, section 1; Vinberg 1972, section 3.2).
Measured at the computed controlling vector `(7,-17,-34,-51,-68,-85,-60,21,4,4)`
(square `-4` in the engine's convention):

- `FundCone()` returns **eight** roots, seven of square `-4` and one of `-2`
  (`.tmp/qc-evidence/issue387-probe-a.log`).  The #362 heartbeat's fourteen walls at 324 s
  are these eight plus six walk additions, not FundCone's output.
- Their pairwise inner products are all nonpositive and none is a nonnegative
  combination of the others (probe A).
- The distance-zero admitted roots number **176** (153 of square `-4`, 23 of
  `-2`), their span has rank **eight** of the rank nine, and **every one of
  them is a same-sign integer combination of FundCone's eight** (probe B,
  `.tmp/qc-evidence/issue387-probe-b.log`): the eight are a fundamental system of the whole
  distance-zero root system.

So the issue's second case holds: the roots are a fundamental system, and the
controlling vector or the group differs from Sterk's cusp 4.

## 3. The group differs, and the measurement says how

Three measurements pin it down:

1. **The walk, with the candidate rules repaired** (below), accepts sixteen
   walls -- eight of square `-4` and eight of `-2` -- and gains none between
   354 s and 1500 s (probe C, `.tmp/qc-evidence/issue387-probe-c.log`).  The same sixteen
   walls come out at the figure's cusp vector `2e' + f' + alpha-bar_1`
   (4.3.8, p. 23), whose reduction the library builds as the same lattice with
   the same computed controlling vector (probe D,
   `.tmp/qc-evidence/issue387-probe-d.log`).
2. **The card's eleven walls** (`Sterk.cusp_roots_in_TEn("Sterk_4")`) are:
   eight that lie in the perpendicular of the (4.2.3) cusp vector -- seven
   consecutive `E8(-2)` generators `a2..a8` and `-e + f` -- all eight admitted
   by the root filter (probes B, F, `.tmp/qc-evidence/issue387-probe-f.log`); and three
   (`alpha_10, alpha_11, alpha_12`) that pair `4, -2, -4` with that cusp vector
   and so name no class of its reduction at all.  All eleven lie in the
   perpendicular of the figure's vector and are admitted by the filter there
   (probe G, `.tmp/qc-evidence/issue387-probe-g.log`).
3. The walk's sixteen walls are neither a superset nor a subset of the card's
   eleven (eight walls of square `-4` against the card's nine), so the two
   chambers are not nested wall by wall.

A chamber of a reflection group determines its wall count, so the card's
eleven-wall chamber and the walk's sixteen-wall chamber belong to different
groups.  The filter admits every wall of both, so the group it defines is
strictly bigger than the card's chamber's group: the criterion
`rho(g) in rho(K_v) rho(Gamma)` is necessary for `g`'s class to lie in the
stabilizer image `G_v` but not sufficient -- sufficiency needs
`ker rho intersect P_v` to lie in `Gamma K_v`, and at this cusp it does not.
The walk's chamber is a tile of the card's chamber, which is why it does not
close on the card's count.

## 4. The candidate-rule repairs (landed with the constructor)

Two defects of the engine's candidate rules bite at this anchor and are
repaired in the same commit:

- `VinAl.IsNewRoot` refuses a candidate linearly dependent on the first `n-1`
  accepted roots.  That equals the distance-zero test only when the initial
  roots span `v0^perp`; here they span rank eight of the rank nine, and the
  engine's span is a foreign rank-nine subspace.  The repair is the
  distance-zero test itself: `FundCone` decided every root at distance zero,
  and no further distance-zero mirror bounds a chamber that coincides with
  that cell near `v0` (Vinberg 1972, section 3.2, the construction after
  Proposition 3, p. 27).
- `VinAl.NextRoot` filters a whole layer against the roots accepted when the
  layer starts and then yields every survivor, so two candidates of one layer
  that pair positively both become walls and a duplicated solution is appended
  twice -- the #362 heartbeat's seventeenth wall was the sixteenth a second
  time.  The repair re-checks each root against the accepted ones before
  yielding it, as Vinberg's sequence (38) does (1972, section 4.1, p. 30).

With these repairs the walk's accepted set is the honest one for the group the
filter defines; the measurements of section 3 are taken with them in place.

## 5. What is not delivered

The Sterk_4 row of the five-row test is not green: `cusp_chamber()` at
Sterk_4 now runs (the constructor returns in milliseconds) and walks, but the
sixteen-wall chamber it computes does not close, and the card's chamber belongs
to a smaller reflection group than the filter's.  The repair that remains is
the filter's exactness: the criterion must decide membership in
`Gamma K_v` and not only in `rho(Gamma) rho(K_v)`, and the place to repair it
is the discriminant criterion of `contains_lift`
(`src/sage_extensions/categories/isotropic_orbits.py`).  No run of the row
through the public call is pasted here beyond the probes above, and the row
has no green test to report.
