# #369 — generators of Õ(L) on card 0125, from Allcock's theorem

Card `0125`, `L = U(2) ⊕ E8(−2)` (rank 10, signature (1,9), `|A_L| = 1024`,
`det = −1024`). The group is `Õ(L) = ker(ρ_L: O(L) → O(A_L))`, the capability the
library names `_MISSING_ARITHMETIC_GENERATING_SET`.

## The citation

**D. Allcock, *Congruence subgroups and Enriques surface automorphisms*,
J. London Math. Soc. (2) 98 (2018), 1–11**, doi:10.1112/jlms.12113,
arXiv:1601.00103v2.

* **Theorem 1.1, printed p. 2.** For `Λ` the E10 lattice `E8 ⊕ U` (`E8` negative
  definite), `O↑(Λ) = W237`, and `W237(2)` is *the level two congruence subgroup,
  meaning the kernel of the natural map `O↑(Λ) → O(V)`*, `V = Λ/2Λ`.
* **Theorem 1.1's proof, printed p. 6.** "The proof amounts to showing that the
  Bertini involutions generate `W237(2)`", a Bertini involution being *the
  transformation which negates the `E8` summand* of a decomposition
  `Λ = E8 ⊕ U`; the same paragraph records that every conjugate of a Bertini
  involution is one, so the group they generate is normal in `O(Λ)`.
* **Theorem 2.2 (Vinberg), printed p. 6.** `O↑(Λ) = W237`, generated by the simple
  reflections of its Coxeter diagram (Vinberg, *On the groups of unit elements of
  certain quadratic forms*, Math. USSR Sb. 16 (1972)).

**Why this is the group of card 0125.** `L = Λ(2)`, so `L` and `Λ` are the same
`Z`-module with forms differing by the factor 2: `O(L) = O(Λ)` as subgroups of
`GL_10(Z)`, and `A_L = Λ/2Λ`, so `Õ(L) = ker(O(Λ) → O(Λ/2Λ))`. That kernel is
`⟨W237(2), −1⟩`: `−1` acts trivially on `Λ/2Λ`, and `O(Λ) = ⟨O↑(Λ), −1⟩` because
`−1` exchanges the two components of the positive cone. Hence

`Õ(L) = ⟨B₀, σᵢ B₀ σᵢ⁻¹ (i = 1..10), −1⟩`, `B₀` one Bertini involution and `σᵢ`
the ten simple reflections: `W237(2)` is the normal closure of `B₀` in
`W237 = ⟨σᵢ⟩`, so it is generated by `B₀` and its conjugates by the generators.

## The hypothesis, checked on `L` by computation

| quantity | value |
| --- | --- |
| rank, signature, determinant | 10, (1,9), −1024 |
| even, 2-elementary | `True`, `True` |
| `\|A_L\|` | 1024 = 2¹⁰ |
| content of the Gram matrix | **2** (every entry even, and 2 occurs) |

The content being 2 is what makes `L = Λ(2)` with `Λ` unimodular; with signature
(1,9) that forces `Λ = II_{1,9} = U ⊕ E8(−1) = E8 ⊕ U`, and therefore the
hypothesis of Allcock's theorem holds for the lattice. The same measurements show
the *other* published generation statements do not reach this lattice, which is
why the search had to go to the level-two statement: the Witt index of `L` is 1,
so Kneser's conditions (Witt index ≥ 2, `rank₂ ≥ 6`) fail, and `L` contains no
hyperbolic plane at all (every pairing is even), so GHS's Propositions 3.3 and 3.4
(arXiv:0810.1614, two hyperbolic planes) and Eichler's criterion are outside their
hypotheses.

## What is not claimed

The call returns a generating family with the statement behind it; it does not
*verify* generation, which is Allcock's theorem (and the normality sentence on
p. 6 of it), not a computation. What is computed is the hypothesis, the
membership of every generator in `ker ρ_L`, and the index against the card.
