## The search: no published source gives a finite generating set

The owner's comment on the issue (2026-11-12) records that the three returned
elements of `5b75461d72` are not shown to generate `Õ(L)`: the docstring
claimed that `W237(2)` is the normal closure of one Bertini involution in
`W237`, "generated by it and its conjugates by the simple reflections" — a
step that is false in general (the normal closure needs all conjugates).

The search this turn took the owner's batch: Allcock's paper was read in
full (arXiv:1601.00103v2, 16 pages, extracted at `.scratch/allcock.txt`),
and the machine's research library (`~/research/references/literature/
lattice-research/`, 18 extractions: Allcock's paper, Barth–Peters,
Brandhorst–Shimada arXiv:2012.10622, Cossec–Dolgachev, Conway–Sloane SPLAG,
Nikulin, Peters–Sterk, Vinberg 1972/1975/1983, and the writing on the Coble
and Enriques moduli programs) was searched, together with arXiv and the web.
The result:

* **Allcock's Theorem 1.1 (p. 2) with its proof (p. 6)** generates `W237(2)`
  by the Bertini involutions — one per decomposition `Λ = E8 ⊕ U`, an
  **infinite family**. The paper gives no finite generating set.
* **The sources it cites** ([1] ATLAS, [2] Barth–Peters, [3] Bourbaki,
  [4] Coble, [5] SPLAG, [6][7][8] Cossec–Dolgachev, [9] Dolgachev,
  [10] Vinberg 1975) give none either: Barth–Peters, Cossec–Dolgachev and
  Nikulin identify the group existentially; Vinberg 1975 is the reflection
  group theory.
* **The other published generation statements do not reach this lattice**:
  Kneser's theorem (as quoted by Gritsenko–Hulek–Sankaran, arXiv:0810.1614,
  Theorem 1.1, p. 4) needs Witt index ≥ 2 and `rank₂(L) ≥ 6`, and `L`'s
  Witt index is 1; GHS's Proposition 3.4 (p. 12) needs two hyperbolic
  planes, and `L` contains none (every pairing is even).
* **The Brandhorst–Shimada computation** (arXiv:2012.10622, "Automorphism
  groups of certain Enriques surfaces") identifies `aut(Y₀)` for the
  (0,0)-generic Enriques surface with the level-two subgroup
  (Theorem 1.17, Barth–Peters [2], Nikulin [23]) and computes finite
  generating sets of `aut(Y)` for the 182 (τ,τ̄)-generic nodal and cuspidal
  cases of its Table 1.1 — but the (0,0)-generic case is not among the
  computed records, and its Theorem 1.17 is an identification, not a
  generation statement.

So the call refuses: it constructs and certifies the family it could return
(each member's discriminant image is trivial) and then raises, stating that
no published generation statement gives a finite generating set of
`Õ(L)` whose hypotheses this lattice meets.
