## The method

`PrimitiveIsotropicSubobjects.pointwise_vector_equivalence_witness` now splits on
whether $I = I^\perp$; the maximal case keeps its previous decision unchanged.

For $I \subsetneq I^\perp$: $C_I$ preserves $I^\perp$ — for $y \in I^\perp$ and
$i \in I$, $b(i, g(y)) = b(g^{-1}(i), y) = b(i, y) = 0$ — and acts on $I^\perp$ as
the identity on $I$, so it descends to $I^\perp/I$. The parabolic exact sequence
already stated at the top of the module therefore reads

$$1 \to U_I \to C_I \to M_I^0 \to 1, \qquad M_I^0 \le O(I^\perp/I),$$

with $U_I = \{g : g|_I = 1,\ (g-1)(I^\perp) \subseteq I\}$ the radical. That is the
$Q_1 = 1$ case of the block form of an isometry preserving a totally isotropic
$k$-plane: in a basis $(u_a \mid p_a \mid \tilde\kappa_b)$ of
$U \mid I \mid I^\perp/I$ the Gram matrix is
$\begin{pmatrix} H & J & 0 \\ J^t & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & A\end{pmatrix}$ with $J$ and
$A$ non-degenerate, and an extending isometry is
$\begin{pmatrix} P_1 & P_2 & P_3 \\ 0 & Q_1 & 0 \\ 0 & Q_2 & Q_3\end{pmatrix}$
subject to $J = P_1 J Q_1^t$, where $Q_1$ is the action on $I$. So $Q_1 = 1$
together with the invertibility of $J$ forces $P_1 = 1$: the $I$-block and the
$U$-block of a pointwise element are the identity, and what remains is $Q_3$, the
action on $I^\perp/I$, and the radical entries $P_2, P_3, Q_2$.

The radical is generated by the Eichler transvections $t(p_a, X)$, $X \in I^\perp$,
whose parameters add, and $t(p_a, X)$ acts on $y \in I^\perp$ by
$y \mapsto y - b(X, y)p_a$ while fixing all of $I$. Prescribing the displacement
inside $I$ is then one integer linear system per $a$ in the coordinates of $X_a$ on
a `ZZ`-basis of $I^\perp$, solved exactly by Smith normal form (`_integral_solution`).

Decided as `None`, each from one of those statements and never from a failed search:

* $q(\text{left}) \ne q(\text{right})$ — every element of $C_I$ is an isometry of $L$;
* $b(\text{left}, p_a) \ne b(\text{right}, p_a)$ for a generator $p_a$ of $I$,
  because $b(g(\text{left}), p_a) = b(\text{left}, g^{-1}(p_a)) = b(\text{left}, p_a)$;
* $\text{right} - \text{left} \notin I^\perp$, because $b(p_a, g(\text{left}) - \text{left}) = 0$;
* both vectors in $I$ and distinct — $C_I$ fixes them individually;
* both in $I^\perp$, $I^\perp/I$ definite, and the two reductions in different
  $O(I^\perp/I)$-orbits — $C_I$ descends into $O(I^\perp/I)$, so it can only carry a
  class into the $O(I^\perp/I)$-orbit of that class. This is a statement about the
  whole orthogonal group of the quotient, not about a selected generating family.

A witness is assembled from the same three factors and evaluated on the first
vector before it is returned:

* `_pointwise_block_isometry(quotient_action)` — the block $\mathrm{diag}(1,1,Q_3)$
  through the change of basis, checked against the lattice's own form test;
* `_pointwise_radical_element(vector, displacement)` — the Eichler shear, one
  integral linear system;
* the pointwise kernel, through `_kernel_parameter_witness`, whose parameter module
  is complete (`IsotropicReductions._pointwise_perpendicular_kernel_data`).

The three statements cited, with their numbers and printed pages:

1. Dutour Sikirić–Hulek, *Moduli of polarised Enriques surfaces — computational
   aspects*, J. London Math. Soc. 110 (2024), arXiv:2302.01679v2, **Theorem 4.9 and
   its proof, printed pp. 33–34**: the block form of an isometry preserving a
   totally isotropic $k$-plane, its blocks $P_1, P_2, P_3, Q_1, Q_2, Q_3$, the
   equations $H = P_1 H P_1^t + \dots$, $J = P_1 J Q_1^t$, $K = P_1 J Q_2^t + P_1 K Q_3^t + P_3 A Q_3^t$,
   and equation (13) for the underdetermined $P_2$.
2. Gritsenko–Hulek–Sankaran, arXiv:0810.1614, **§3.1, Lemma 3.1 and equations (4)–(5),
   printed p. 8**: $t(e,a)(v) = v - (a,v)e + (e,v)a - \tfrac12(a,a)(e,v)e$ and
   $t(e,a)t(e,b) = t(e,a+b)$, $t(e,a)^{-1} = t(e,-a)$. The additivity of the
   parameter in (5) is what makes the shear system linear.
3. The complete integral parameter module of the pointwise kernel, as derived at
   `IsotropicReductions._pointwise_perpendicular_kernel_data` in
   `categories/lattices.py` (the isometry equations $b(Nf_a,f_b) + b(f_a,Nf_b) = 0$).

Each of the three now appears in the docstring of the method or of the helper that
runs it.

## Boundary: what is still undecided, and what is done about it

Three refusals, each naming its missing datum, and no `None` among them:

* a vector outside $I^\perp$: its image under the action on a complement of
  $I^\perp$ is not parametrized here, although that action is trivial on
  $L/I^\perp$. Both vectors must lie in $I^\perp$;
* an indefinite $I^\perp/I$ — for instance $U \oplus U \oplus K$ at an isotropic
  *line*, where $I^\perp/I$ keeps a hyperbolic plane. The isometry group of the
  quotient is then infinite and its orbits are not a finite computation. The last
  test of the module asserts this refusal;
* a displacement the transvection parameters do not reach integrally, and an odd
  ambient lattice, where the coefficient $q(X)/2$ of equation (4) is not integral.

A witness that the search found is always verified on the endpoints; where the
search is exhausted without one the call refuses rather than answering `None`.

## Two defects found on the way

1. **The perpendicular of a non-maximal isotropic subobject could not be built at
   all.** `perpendicular_basis` computed `self.plane_basis()`, which requires rank
   two, and `splitting_complement` stopped its search at two vectors. The plane
   assumption belongs to the Levi-block construction (`_levi_block_isometry`,
   `levi_lift_generators`, written for a $2 \times 2$ block), not to the
   perpendicular. Both now read the new `isotropic_basis()` and take the
   subobject's own rank; at rank two the answers are unchanged. Recorded in
   `TRAPS.md`.
2. **The pointwise decision asserted its hypothesis** instead of deciding the case
   the issue is about; that assert is gone and the general case is decided above.

## Not done here

The action of $C_I$ on a complement of $I^\perp$ is not implemented, so a pair
outside $I^\perp$ refuses rather than being decided; and the shear family is the
Eichler family, not a proof that every integral element of $U_I$ is one of its
composites. Neither is claimed.
