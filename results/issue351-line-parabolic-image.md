# #351: the (ρ, sn)-image of the stabilizer of a primitive isotropic vector

## The consumer

When #351 closes, a public call returns the image
`(ρ, sn)(Stab_{O(N)}(v)) ⊆ O(q_N) × {±1}` for a primitive isotropic vector `v` of an even
indefinite lattice `N`, **without enumerating a generating family of `Stab_{O(N)}(v)` first**
— the input #17's double-coset route needs and cannot obtain on the Enriques period lattice
(`results/issue17-double-coset-row.md`: 11052 generators, 2090 s and 3.1 GiB for the first of
the two `O(N)`-line representatives, through the port's marked-lattice route).

## The statement, and its sign conventions

The structure of the stabilizer of an isotropic line as an extension by the unipotent radical
of the parabolic — **S. Zemel, *The Structure of Integral Parabolic Subgroups of Orthogonal
Groups*, J. Algebra 559 (2020) 95–128, arXiv:1902.08264v2**:

* **Corollary 3.6 (p. 26 of the arXiv v2)** — for `I = ZZ v` with `v` primitive isotropic in
  the even lattice `L`, and `U = QQ v`, the intersection `Γ_{L,I} = Γ_L ∩ 𝒫_U` of the
  discriminant kernel `Γ_L` with the parabolic is the semi-direct product `Λ ⋊ Γ_Λ`, where
  `Λ = (I^⊥ ∩ L)/I = v^⊥ ∩ L / ZZ v` — **the lattice `v^⊥/v`** — and `Γ_Λ` is the
  discriminant kernel of `Λ` (its intersection with the connected component `SO⁺` is
  Zemel's convention: `Γ_L = ker Aut(L) → Aut(Δ_L) ∩ SO⁺(V_R)`).
* **Proposition 1.10 (p. 13)** — the sign conventions of the joint character on the
  parabolic: for an element of `𝒫_U` with Levi part `(M, γ) ∈ GL(U) × O(W)` (where
  `W = U^⊥/U`), **the determinant is `det γ` and the real spinor norm is the class of
  `det M` times the spinor norm of `γ`**. For the pointwise stabilizer of the vector `v`
  the `GL(U)`-part is trivial, so the sign is carried by the `O(v^⊥/v)`-part alone.
* **Section 2, the paragraph following Definition 2.1 (p. 15)** — the discriminant data of
  `v`: the subgroup `H_I = (L + I_{L*})/L ⊆ Δ_L` and the identification
  `Δ_Λ ≅ H_I^⊥ / H_I`. For `div(v) = 1` the subgroup `H_I` is trivial and
  `Δ_Λ = Δ_L`; for `div(v) = 2` the subgroup `H_I` is generated by the divided class
  `[v/2]`.

The consequence for the image: `(ρ, sn)(Stab_{O(L)}(v))` is the quotient of the parabolic by
its kernel part `Λ ⋊ Γ_Λ`, hence the product of the image of the unipotent radical's
complement (the shear quotient) with **the image of the Levi factor in `O(A_{v^⊥/v})`**,
transported to `A_L` through the discriminant data of `v`. That is the route the issue
names: the image is computed from `O(v^⊥/v)` and the discriminant data of `v`.

For the Enriques period lattice the index of the Levi image is published — **D. Dutour
Sikirić and K. Hulek, *Moduli of polarised Enriques surfaces — computational aspects*,
J. London Math. Soc. 110 (2024), arXiv:2302.01679v2, §3.6, p. 21**: "The stabilizer of `L1`
has an image in the discriminant group which is equal to the full group, while the image of
the stabilizer of `L2 has index 527 = 17 · 31", with `L1 = ZZ e1` and `L2 = ZZ e3` — the two
`O(N)`-orbits of isotropic lines. The count per representative is that index, and the row is
`1 + 527 = 528`, the card's `Otilde+` value.

## Where the route stops

The owner is `OrthogonalCharacterQuotient.isotropic_line_stabilizer_image`
(`src/sage_extensions/categories/orthogonal_quotients.py:1100`), reached by
`_subgroup_isotropic_orbit_representatives` at `:2345`. The Eichler route through it is
gated at `:1171` by `_two_hyperbolic_planes(lattice)`, which `N` fails (`U(2)` is a scaled
hyperbolic plane: `b(e3, f3) = 2`, not `±1`), so the call returns `None` and the row falls
through to the port enumeration. Replacing the gate by the parabolic route requires two
certificates per representative, and the second one is the stop:

1. **the class-stabilizer equality** `ρ(Stab_{O(N)}(v)) = Stab_{ρ(O(N))}({c, −c})` with
   `c = [v/div v]` — the inclusion always holds (`g(v) = v` implies `ρ(g)` fixes `[v/d]`),
   and on `N` the equality is forced by Dutour Sikirić–Hulek's published indices (1 and 527,
   §3.6 p. 21) agreeing with the computed class orbit: equal orders and the inclusion give
   the equality. This certificate is available.
2. **the sign witness** — an element of `Stab(v) ∩ ker ρ` with real spinor norm `−1`, which
   doubles the joint image `K` and makes the count per representative equal the published
   index. **Here the route stops**: for the divisor-1 representative `L1 = ZZ e1`, every
   vector orthogonal to `e1` has norm in `4ZZ` — with `b(x, e1)` the `f1`-coefficient, the
   orthogonal vectors have `f1`-coefficient `0`, so their norm is `4cd − 4k ∈ 4ZZ`, never
   `2` — so **no root reflection fixes `L1`**, and the witness must be a non-reflection
   element (a product of a non-orthogonal reflection with a transvection, for example).
   The library's witness searches enumerate root reflections only
   (`vectors_of_square_and_divisibility(2, 1)` with `b(root, vector) = 0`, at `:1236` and
   `:1252`), so they find nothing at `L1` and the zero-class branch correctly refuses. For
   the divisor-2 representative `L2 = ZZ e3` the root witness does exist (`e1 + f1` is a
   norm-2, divisor-1 root orthogonal to `e3`), so the stop is specifically the divisor-1
   line.

## What is missing

A construction of the non-reflection sign witness at the divisor-1 line — an element of
`Stab(e1) ∩ ker ρ` with real spinor norm `−1` — or an extension of the witness search
beyond root reflections. With that witness, the wiring is: the class-stabilizer equality
(certificate 1) plus the doubled joint image (certificate 2) give the count per
representative as the class-orbit size (1 and 527), the row `1 + 527 = 528` — the card's
`Otilde+` value read by tag — and `_subgroup_isotropic_orbit_representatives`'s third
branch routes through `splitting_isometries_from_line_class` instead of the port
enumeration. The count identity itself is not in doubt: with the card's own indices
(1 and 527, Dutour Sikirić–Hulek §3.6 p. 21) the double-coset formula gives 528 exactly.
