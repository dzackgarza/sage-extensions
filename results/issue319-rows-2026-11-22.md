## The ledger, and the aggregate that cannot skip a row

Every attempt now lands in one durable file,
`results/issue319-rows-ledger.jsonl`: one JSON line a row, with the row and its
card, the dimension (the quantity, and the degree for card `0128`), the value the
public call returned **or the exception it raised**, the state, the elapsed time,
the revision and where the line came from.  It is append-only and
`results/issue319-merge-row-ledger.py` is what appends to it, from a run's printed
log or from its `rows-<revision12>-*.jsonl` record, skipping a line it already
holds.  A row that raised keeps `"returned": null` and its `"detail"`; nothing
turns an exception or a non-return into a value.

The module gains two flags, and the second is the no-skip rule:

* `--ledger PATH` appends every attempted row of the run to the ledger.
* `--coverage PATH` computes nothing: it reads the ledger, and reports coverage
  **per dimension** — `0128 #h at 2d = 8`, `029J planes`, `gamma-h-9 flags` — then
  names every required row whose latest attempt there is not a `PASS`, and exits
  1.  A row with no attempt at all is absent; a row whose latest attempt is
  `BROKEN`, `UNDECIDED`, `MISSING` or `NOT REACHED` is absent too.  With no
  selection it is the whole 349-row acceptance, so a batch can no longer stand in
  for the acceptance: it reports what it decided and names what it did not.

`tests/engineering/test_the_aggregate_comparison_names_an_absent_row.py` decides
that rule on ledgers the test writes: a held `PASS` is a pass, an empty ledger
names the row `ABSENT otilde-plus-t-en:lines` and exits 1, and a line recording a
raised exception stays absent rather than counting as covered.  3 passed, 4.96 s,
with the module's peer test beside it: 5 passed, 8.57 s.

### The rule this changed: agreement is per revision, not per last line

`--coverage` read a row's *latest* attempt, which would have let the two passing
re-runs erase the attempt that raised.  It now reads **every** attempt a row has at
the latest revision it was tried at, and decides the row only when all of them are
`PASS` with the same number:

* any attempt that is not a `PASS` at that revision leaves the row absent, naming
  how many of how many;
* two attempts that return different numbers leave it absent as well — two answers
  from one operation at one revision are not a decision.

`gamma-h-2:flags` is therefore absent with `1 of 3 attempts at 56f0ec868006 are not
PASS`, which is the true state of that row.  Two tests decide the new rule, one for
each clause, beside the three that decide the older one: 5 passed in 19.40 s.
