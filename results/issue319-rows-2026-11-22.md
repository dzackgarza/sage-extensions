each one public call of #286 compared with a card's own value read by tag.  This
note carries rows that returned at the revision `main` is at
(`97f2f72507c52d743f742ac4e1af2975be0780f1`), and nothing else: a row's value is what

`expected … call …` line, and the `@@ROW@@` object for a single-row run — and
those blocks are in `results/issue319-rows-2026-11-22.log` beside this file.  A row that did not
return is named as not returned; no count of rows stands in for a row.

## Which workflow owns the remaining rows

Two workflows were on `main` and both were scheduled, both running this one
module:

| workflow | schedule | shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `enriques-polarization-rows.yml` | `17 3 * * *` | one job, every row, `--budget 18000` |
| `enriques-polarization-rows-full.yml` | `43 3 * * *` | 20 jobs, one per card or card group, `--cap 1800` each |

`enriques-polarization-rows-full.yml` owns the remaining rows: its twenty
`--tags` entries are 92 distinct tags — the 87 `gamma-h-N` and the five card tags
— with no gap and no duplicate, so every row the module enumerates is inside one
of its jobs, and each job carries a budget sized to its own cards.
`enriques-polarization-rows.yml` runs the same module over the same rows in one
job under a budget that cannot hold them: its own predecessor run 44924240209
spent 19200 s and stopped at row 36 of 349, reporting 313 rows not reached.  It
is a duplicate of the current path with no row of its own, so this change removes
it (`git rm .github/workflows/enriques-polarization-rows.yml`); nothing else in
the tree names it.  `just enriques-card-rows` runs the same module with a
selection from any checkout, which is what the removed workflow's dispatch
inputs offered.

Two things about the surviving matrix belong to it rather than to this change,
and are named here because they decide whether its jobs can reach their rows:

* its `0128` job is one job of all 72 Table 4 rows under `--budget 19200`, and
  the `#Gamma_h` rows cost 372 to 887 s each at `2d = 4` to `22` and more as the
  class count grows, so that job cannot reach its own high degrees.  The 0128
  split by degree range, with the `#h` rows in a job of their own, is in the
  draft `results/issue319-enriques-polarization-rows.workflow.yml` and has not
  been installed into `.github/workflows/`; installing it is a workflow write,
  which this connection does not hold.
* every `gamma-h-N` job is one job of three rows per card, and the flag row costs
  the ambient `O(T_En)` flag row (82 to 150 s) at each of the row's calls.
