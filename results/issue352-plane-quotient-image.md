# #352: the image of the plane stabilizer in O(P^⊥/P) at Sterk's P₂, and its index

## The statement, and why no citation was needed

The unit asked for a cited statement identifying the image of the whole setwise stabilizer of
P₂ in O(P₂^⊥/P₂), *or* a computation of it.  It is **computed** here, and the computation is a
two-line argument that needs no source:

* every generator returned by `parabolic_stabilizer_generators` is an isometry with `g(P) = P`,
  so its Levi image lies in the image M of the whole setwise stabilizer;
* that image lies in O(P^⊥/P) by definition.

So `H₀ ⊆ M ⊆ O(P^⊥/P)` (`H₀` the generated subgroup of the returned images), and **equal
orders force both inclusions to be equalities**.  The candidate sources were checked and are
about something else: Dutour Sikirić–Hulek, arXiv:2302.01679v2, Theorem 4.9 and its proof,
printed **p. 22**, are about the *existence* of an integral lift of a prescribed quotient
action (an algorithm, with a finite-index restriction), and they name no generating set of the
parabolic; Scattone's Proposition 3.7.3, as they cite it, generates the radical.  Neither is a
statement about M, and the sandwich above does not need one.

The only external data used are the plane and the bases: H. Sterk,
*Compactifications of the period space of Enriques surfaces, Part I*, Math. Z. 207 (1991),
Proposition 4.6, p. 29 for `P₂ = Z(2e₁+2e₂+w) + Ze₃`, in the bases of Dutour Sikirić–Hulek
§3.6, p. 22.

## The image and the index at P₂

Measured on this tree (`.scratch/issue352_orders.py`, `.scratch/issue352_stabfamily.py`):

| quantity | value |
| --- | --- |
| `K = P₂^⊥/P₂` | rank 8, signature `(0,8)`, determinant 64 |
| `\|O(K)\|` | **10 321 920** (0.2 s, the engine's own order) |
| parabolic family | 122 generators |
| `\|H₀\|` from the parabolic family | **10 321 920** — all of `O(K)` |
| `L.Aut().stabilizer(P, setwise)` family | 118 generators (59.3 s) |
| `\|H₀\|` from that family | **10 321 920** — all of `O(K)` |
| **index** `[O(K) : H₀]` | **1** |

So at P₂ the image of the whole setwise stabilizer in `O(P₂^⊥/P₂)` is the whole group, and the
returned generators' images are that image.  The second row is the direct identification of M
(the stabilizer's returned family generates the stabilizer by construction, so its images
generate M), the first is the same statement for the parabolic family that
`is_parabolic_complete()` judges.

## The code site

`src/sage_extensions/categories/isotropic_parabolics.py`, at the owner of
`is_parabolic_complete()` (the plane's parabolic `ParentMethods`):

* **new** `levi_quotient_image_index()` — returns `[O(I^⊥/I) : H₀]`, where H₀ is the subgroup
  generated by the images of `parabolic_stabilizer_generators()` under
  `IsotropicReductions.levi_action()`.  It states the sandwich in its docstring and says what
  each value means: `1` proves `H₀ = M = O(I^⊥/I)`; a larger value is the bound
  `[O : H₀]` and does **not** identify M.  A non-dividing pair of orders raises (Lagrange).
* `is_parabolic_complete()`'s docstring: the "not split" bullet now records that the plane
  action and the quotient action are both decided, and that the radical is the factor that
  remains.
* `parabolic_obstruction()`: the returned text now reports the quotient factor as decided,
  with the three measured numbers (`[|O(K)| : |H₀|]`, the index, and whether M is identified),
  and names only the radical as undecided.

`is_parabolic_complete()` itself still returns `False` at P₂: the radical is #353's, and this
unit does not touch it.
