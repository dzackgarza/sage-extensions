# #603: certified generators of a primitive vector's stabilizer, item by item

The issue asks for a public call that returns, for a primitive vector `v` of an
indefinite lattice `L`, generators of `Stab_{O(L)}(v)` built by the library's own
construction as the line and plane stabilizers are since `58588b8c83` (#459), and
for that call to reproduce a lattice-database card value on a card with a cited
or certified stabilizer.  Every number below is from a run on the adopted runtime
in a worktree of `omm/main = d667a7cb42` (tree bound at `/repos/sage-extensions`,
the port at `/work`, time gates on); no count is typed except where it is a card
value read by tag.

## 1. The call, and the construction behind it

`O(L).stabilizer(v)` and `O(L).vector_stabilizer_generators(v)` are the pointed
marking's entry points; they read the apex image of `Aut` of `<q(v)> -> L` and now
reach the lattice category's own construction.

`_pointed_vector_stabilizer_generators` (`src/sage_extensions/categories/lattices.py`,
called from the dispatch in `_marked_automorphism_generators` beside the plane and
line slots) builds `Stab(v) = P_I^1 = ker(P_I -> O(I))` for `I = Zv` from the
rank-one parabolic `P_I`'s family that #459 constructs
(`_isotropic_line_stabilizer_generators`: one lift of each generator of
`O(K_I)`, `K_I = v^perp/Zv`, together with the kernel's selected generators),
decorated by the sign of the action on `v`:

    family = { g in P_I : g(v) = v } union { g_0 g : g in P_I, g(v) = -v },

with `g_0` one fixed reversing member.  That generates the kernel of the sign:
a word with an even number of reversing letters is a product of the `g_0 g` and
`g_0^2`, and `g_1 g_2 = (g_1 g_0)(g_0 g_0)(g_0 g_2)`.  Every member is checked to
fix `v`; a member carrying `v` anywhere but `+-v` raises rather than being
dropped.

At `K_I = 0` the perpendicular is the line itself and the stabilizer is the
integral pointwise perpendicular kernel
(`pointwise_perpendicular_kernel`): the homogeneous parameter module's
generators, integer linear algebra, no isometry search.  This is the rank at
which the setwise construction declines.

Returned family, measured:

| lattice | `v` | `K_I` | family | time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `026E` = `U + D4(-1) + A1(-1)`, rank 7, sig (1,6) | the `U` generator, `q = 0`, `div = 1` | `D4(-1) + A1(-1)`, negative definite | 10 generators | 2.1 s |
| `N = U + U(2) + E8(-2)` (`029J`), rank 12, sig (2,10) | `e` (`Ze_1`), `q = 0`, `div = 1` | rank 10 | 25 generators | 12.2 s |
| `U` (`0016`), rank 2, sig (1,1) | `e`, `q = 0`, `div = 1` | 0 | empty | 0.3 s |

Completeness in the Levi factor on `026E`: the image of the 10 generators in
`O(K_I)` generates a subgroup of order 2304, which is all of `O(K_I)`.  (The
port's family there has 22 generators and the same image, so on this specimen the
construction is a smaller family for the same content.)

| `026E` | `integral.primitive_orbits`, `O` and `SO` | 1, 1 | `isotropic_orbit_representatives(1).cardinality()` on `O(L)` and on `SO(L)`: a test |
| `0016` | `integral.primitive_orbits`, `O` | 1 | same call on `U`: a test |
| `029J` | `O` row citation: the two orbits `L_1 = Ze_1`, `L_2 = Ze_3` "with images of their stabilizers in the discriminant group of index 1 and 527" | 1 for `L_1` | `[O(A_N) : <rho(family at Ze_1), rho(-id)>]`: measured, not a test |

The `029J` number is the card's cited stabilizer index, and the family's image is
the stabilizer's: `P_{L_1}` is generated by the pointed family at `e` together
with `-id`, which negates `e` and stabilizes every line.  Measured index: 1, the
card's value for `L_1`.

**Why that row is not a test.** The call measures 14.23 s in a quiet suite run and
23.00 s under load, over the suite's 20 s call ceiling
(`sage_extensions.testing.suite_budget`), which makes the run red.  Under
`--no-time-gates` it passes; with the gates on it is a red gate rather than red
mathematics, so it is recorded here instead.

## 3. The red: where the port's route is the defect

The issue's defect is the *absence* of a constructed family, and the port's
pointed families are correct wherever they return inside the suite's ceiling:
all four tests of
`tests/lattices/test_the_pointed_stabilizer_of_a_primitive_vector.sage` pass at
the parent `d667a7cb42` as well, including the two card rows the file asserts.
There is no red test for the construction on those specimens, and none was
manufactured.

The specimen where the port's route is a defect is the card-bearing Enriques
period lattice, where it does not return:

* parent `d667a7cb42`, `NamedLattices.TEn`, `v = e`, standalone probe
  (`red400.py` under `.tmp/launch582.sh`, inner `timeout -s KILL 400`): printed
  `parent d667a7c: TEn at e, q = 0 div 1` and nothing more; `exit: 124`, the
  400 s kill.  An earlier, shorter probe was stopped at 117 s inside the same
  call.
* parent, same test file: `3 passed`, the fourth test not returned at 123 s
  (stopped).
* this branch: 25 generators in 12.2 s, all fixing `e`.

Two further library answers fail at the parent and at this branch alike, and are
**not** repaired here: `U.SO().isotropic_orbit_representatives(1)` raises
`ArithmeticError` ("no exhibited stabilizer element closes the index 2") while
card `0016` states 2 `SO`-orbits of primitive isotropic lines.  That failure is in
the *setwise* stabilizer's character image
(`exact_isotropic_stabilizer_generators`, `upper` order 2 against `lower` order
1), not in the pointed family, so it is another issue's.

## 4. Boundaries stated, not crossed

* A **non-isotropic** primitive `v` is answered with `None` and keeps the port's
  route: `v^perp/Zv` does not exist, the stabilizer is the isometries of
  `v^perp \cap L` preserving the glue with `Zv`, and that construction is not
  owned in this tree.  `026E` at its norm-`-2` generator still returns the port's
  8 generators, unchanged.
* A coslice at a sublattice of rank two and up is the pointwise stabilizer of a
  plane, and an oriented plane is the determinant kernel on the plane: both keep
  the port's route, as before.
* A base ring other than `ZZ`, an odd lattice, or a nonprimitive vector: `None`.
* `T_Co` and research#760's cusp-5 root orbits are not run here; the call they
  need is the one this issue adds, at `K_I` of rank 9.
