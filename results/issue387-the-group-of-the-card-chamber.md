## 2. The library's group is that group (measured on main)

Sterk's Proposition 2.7 characterizes `ρ(Γ)` as the decomposition-preserving
subgroup `D = O(A_{H(2)}) × O(A_{E8(−2)})` of `O(A_{T_En})`, concretely the
subgroup generated by the discriminant images of the block isometries of
`T_En` that preserve each block: the swap `e' ↔ f'` of the `U(2)` block and the
eight reflections in the roots `a_i` of the `E8(−2)` block (the image of the
`E8` Weyl group on `E8/2E8` is `O^+(8,2)`, and the swap generates
`O(A_{U(2)})`; the two factors commute).

Probe `issue387-probe-m.log` measures, at the fetched head `118a910`:

## 3. `contains_lift` is exact for this group

The mathematics the issue asks for in its second item, proved:

Sterk's Proposition 2.7 makes `Γ` a **discriminant-characterized** group:
`Γ = ρ^{-1}(ρ(Γ))`, so `ker ρ ≤ Γ`.  The kernel `K_v` of the Levi map lies in
`ker ρ` — measured, `|ρ(K_v)| = 1` at Sterk's cusp 4 (probe
`issue387-probe-m.log`), and the library's own docstring records it trivial at
all five cusps.  Then for any isometry `g` fixing `v`:

`λ(g) ∈ G_v  ⟺  ∃γ ∈ Γ ∩ P_v : λ(γ) = λ(g)  ⟺  γ^{-1}g ∈ K_v  ⟹ ρ(g) = ρ(γ) ∈ ρ(Γ)  ⟹ g ∈ Γ`

and conversely `g ∈ Γ ∩ P_v` gives `λ(g) ∈ G_v` by definition.  So for lifts
fixing `v`, the criterion `ρ(g) ∈ ρ(K_v)ρ(Γ)` decides **exactly** the
membership `λ(g) ∈ G_v`.  The gap the issue names — `ker ρ ∩ P_v ⊆
Stab_Γ(v)·K_v` — holds **trivially** for this group, because `K_v ≤ ker ρ ≤ Γ`
already.  The hypothesis that `contains_lift` admits reflections outside
`G_v` is therefore false for Sterk's `Γ`: no change to the criterion is owed.

## 4. The walk: what the probes show, and the named repairs

With the group right and the filter exact, the 16-vs-11 discrepancy the
previous chat reported is a defect of the **walk**, and the probes pin down
two mechanisms:

* **The stall.**  Probe `issue387-probe-j2.log` (a clean 40-minute run of the
  repaired walk at the computed controlling vector) shows the walk accepts its
  eight additions at heights `√2, √2, 2√2, 4, 3√2, 3√2, 4√2, 4√2` and then
  gains no wall for thirty minutes: it is stuck inside one layer of the
  engine's whole-lattice `qsolve` enumeration.  The library's own
  cone-restricted enumeration (`_FilteredVinAl.Roots_decomposed_into`,
  Vinberg 1972 §4.1 conditions (58)) is **bypassed** at this controlling
  vector, because `cell()` returns `None` there: the cell at the cusp
  controlling vector has *lineality* (its eight normals span rank 8 of the
  nine-dimensional `v0^⊥`, not a basis), and the cone enumeration is
  implemented only for cells whose normals are a basis.
* **The count.**  Vinberg's sequence (38) legitimately contains positive
  roots — every root pairing nonpositively with the accepted ones is appended
  in distance order (Proposition 4's rules (40)–(41)) — and the polyhedron is
  the intersection over the whole sequence, so the extra halfspaces are
  harmless for the polyhedron but **not for the wall count**: a completed walk
  at a cusp cell would report more walls than the chamber has.  The walls of
  the chamber are exactly the **extreme rays of the cone generated by the
  sequence** (each sequence element is a nonnegative combination of the walls,
  and the walls are the extreme rays of the dual cone), so the count the
  five-row test asserts must be taken on the extreme rays, not the sequence
  length.  At Sterk_1–3 the sequence happens to close before any positive root
  is appended, which is why those rows read exact counts today.

The two repairs, at their owners:

1. **Extend the cone enumeration to cells with lineality** —
   `hyperbolic_lattices.py`, `_FilteredVinAl.cell` and
   `Roots_decomposed_into`: parametrize `V_1` by the eight normals plus the
   lineality direction `m` (orthogonal to the normals, positive norm), write
   the layer's norm equation as a quadratic form in `(d, t)` with `d ≥ 0` the
   cone coordinates and `t ∈ Z` the lineality coordinate (the Gram is
   block-diagonal since `m ⊥` normals), and enumerate by the same
   completion-of-squares descent with a two-sided bound on `t`.  This makes
   the layers cheap at cusp cells and lets the walk run past height `2√2·2`.
2. **Report the chamber's walls as the facets of the final polyhedron** —
   filter the accepted sequence to the extreme rays of the cone it generates
   (`Cone(roots).extreme_rays()`), which is Vinberg's Proposition 4 statement
   that `P = ⋂Π_{e_i}⁻` and the walls of `P` are the hyperplanes that bound it.

With both repairs the Sterk_4 row should return the card's eleven walls
through the public call, and the Sterk_1–3 rows are unchanged (their
sequences already close without positive roots, so the extreme-ray filter
removes nothing there).
