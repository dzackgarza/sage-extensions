## Item 2 — the plane class index, returned and recorded

`OrthogonalCharacterQuotient.plane_class_index(plane)` returns
`(J, |Stab_{O(q)}(J)|, |rho(Stab_Gamma(P))|, index)`: `J = [P/2]` is the
order-four totally isotropic subgroup the plane's divided classes span, the
right-hand order is `chi(Gamma) cap chi(Pi)` read in the shared permutation
model from the certified parabolic's own keys (the two groups the covering is
computed from, Dutour Sikirić–Hulek, Lemma 3.6, printed p. 22), and the
left-hand order is `O(q)`'s stabilizer of the class set.  The driver records it
at the row's plane classes — `--plane-class-index N`, default 8, `0` for none —
and prints the three numbers and the index per class, so the value is returned
per class rather than implied.  A row of `T_Co` has 189720 plane cusps over
23715 classes; the bound is stated in the log line.

Tested on `U + U(2)`
(`tests/lattices/test_the_plane_class_index_of_a_coble_plane.sage`): the
stabilizer's order is the image's times the index with no remainder, 21.2 s
under a loaded VM (load 16) against the 20 s gate — marginal there, not on an
idle host.

## Item 3 — the drivers record, and no longer assert, the page's numbers

`tests/coble_gamma_co_2_incidence_rows.py` no longer gates on any stated value.
What it does now:

* the row's degree sequence is printed for every row;
* the Coble kernel rows' degrees are `record_comparison`-ed against the
  integral archive's 1080 and 3, and the run-level counts of `O` (one edge),
  `Gamma_{Co,2}` (13, 4, 7) and the two kernel rows (569160, 527, 189720)
  against their sources, each printed with `agrees` or `DIFFERS`;
* nothing in that path returns a status: the row's `RowStatus` is reached with
  its own contract only (endpoints are cusps, no repeated pair, every cusp
  covered), and the run exits 0 on a decided row whatever the comparison says.

What stays a condition is mathematics, not a stated number: the containment
`Otilde^+ <= Otilde` forcing its cusp counts to be at least as large, and the
trivial-image agreement of a kernel row with the ambient row.

Test: `tests/engineering/test_a_page_value_is_recorded_and_never_decides_the_row.py`
asks the comparison directly — `False` for the archive's kernel count against a
one-edge relation, `True` for a degree of three — and asserts the row that
disagrees is still `DECIDED` with its own counts, 8.33 s.
