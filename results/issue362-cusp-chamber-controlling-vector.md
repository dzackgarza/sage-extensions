## 1. The citation

E. B. Vinberg, *On the groups of unit elements of certain quadratic forms*,
Math. USSR Sb. 16 (1972), §3.2:

- the construction after Proposition 3 (p. 27): take an arbitrary point
  `p_0`, let `Γ_0` be the subgroup generated by the reflections whose mirrors
  pass through it, and let `P_0` be a cell of that subgroup; there is a unique
  cell `P` of `Γ` coinciding with `P_0` near `p_0`;
- Proposition 4 (p. 28): the polyhedron cut out by the normals of the sequence
  (38) is that cell `P`;
- Proposition 5 (p. 29): if the volume of `P^{(m)}` is finite the sequence
  breaks off at the `m`-th term and `P^{(m)} = P`.

A vertex of the chamber of `W(N)` lies in the chamber of every reflection
subgroup of `W(N)` — a group generated by fewer reflections cuts out a bigger
polyhedron — so the search anchored at such a vertex computes the subgroup's
chamber by the three statements above, and no separate certificate is needed.
The docstring of `cusp_chamber` and of `_computed_controlling_vector` carry
this citation with the pages.

## 3. The measurement at Sterk_1, and the repair

One instrumented run of the filtered walk at Sterk_1, anchored at the computed
controlling vector (`.scratch/wt362/p362_probe4.py`, the walk copied from
`_vinal_vinberg_roots` with per-step timing added):

- 20 layers, 1,855,003 candidates, 12 walls (the card row), complete;
- wall time 587.1 s, of which the layer enumeration 72.4 s (12%);
- the candidate filter outside the enumeration 504.3 s (88%), of which:
  - the engine's root test `VinAl.IsRoot` **337.8 s** (57% of the wall time,
    0.18 ms per candidate over 1,854,761 calls);
  - the dependence check `VinAl.are_linearly_dependent` 92.9 s (16%);
  - the wall conditions 36.0 s;
  - the subgroup sift 0.9 s over 5 calls (the coordinate-keyed cache of
    `cusp_chamber` answers every repeated root, so only 5 distinct roots
    reach it).

The step that takes the time is the engine's root test, and its owner in the
library is the call site `_FilteredVinAl.IsNewRoot`
(`src/sage_extensions/categories/hyperbolic_lattices.py`): the repair replaces
the engine's spell with its exact equivalent, one contraction of the candidate
against the engine's Gram matrix and ten modular checks on the result, and
replaces the dependence check, when the cell exists, with the single inner
product `b(candidate, v_0) = 0` (the span of the first accepted roots is
`v_0^⊥` exactly then).  The accepted set is the same; only the cost of asking
changes.
