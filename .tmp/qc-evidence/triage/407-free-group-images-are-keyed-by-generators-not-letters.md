A morphism out of a free group refuses the images of its letters

`F = Groups.Free(2)`, `a, b = F.group_generators()`, `F.Mor(H)({a: h1, b: h2})` --- the
statement of the free group's universal property, in the shape the library's own
`group_generators()` hands a caller --- raises:

    AssertionError: the dictionary does not define a homomorphism out of Group generated by
    Group generators: [0 -> g_0, 1 -> g_1]: it must give an image for every element of the
    free basis {0,...,1}, but it has keys (g_0, g_1)

When this closes, a dictionary keyed by the free group's generators defines its morphism, and
`a -> (1 2)`, `b -> (1 2 3)` is the epimorphism onto `S_3` whose image has order 6.

Observed on `main` at `9b29712` (2026-11-14) in the published image, running the
repository's own invocation one file at a time with the gates this triage lifts:

    just test --no-time-gates --no-test-lint <file>

    src/sage_extensions/categories/group/groups.py:4470: AssertionError

The constructor checks the dictionary's keys against the free *basis labels* `{0, 1}` while
its own message, and `group_generators()`, speak of the letters.
