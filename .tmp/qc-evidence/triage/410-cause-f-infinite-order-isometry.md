The cyclic subgroup of an infinite-order lattice isometry has no cardinality

When this closes, `g.cyclic_subgroup().cardinality()` decides for a lattice isometry `g`: an
isometry of trace greater than 2 on a form of signature (1, 1) has infinite order, so its cyclic
subgroup is countably infinite (`aleph_0`), and a finite-order `g` gives its order. A user asking
for the subgroup generated by an isometry gets its cardinality instead of a refusal that neither
an order algorithm nor a finite ambient enumeration was supplied.
