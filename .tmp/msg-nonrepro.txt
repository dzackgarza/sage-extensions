results: the divided-class key is not reproducible across processes at T_Co (#381)

Theorem first: for Gamma = ker chi, Gamma acts trivially on A_L and the divided
class is rho-equivariant (the equivariance _isotropic_line_image_key's own
docstring states), so the class pair is constant on a Gamma-orbit of lines and
the cusp-to-class-pair map is a function of the cusp.  The partition of the cusps
by key is therefore determined by the lattice and the subgroup.

Computation: it is not.  Otilde at T_Co, same tree, same subgroup, three
processes:

  line-cusps.log   527 cusps, 444 keys, 24 ambiguous, largest 15,12,12,11,11,9,3,2
  fibre-index.log  527 cusps, 351 keys, 104 ambiguous, all of size 2
  key-stability.log  the walk raised: "the orbit walk's transversal element does
                     not carry the start to the reached point" (:2313)

Order of construction is ruled out: building the character image before and
after the cusps, in one process with the cache cleared, both answer 527 cusps
(order2.log, 149.84 s and 68.51 s).

So the count is stable and the partition is not.  By the theorem the partition is
determined, so either the key is not computing the invariant its docstring names,
or the 527 admitted cusps are not one representative per orbit.  Either way the
refusal's trigger is not a property of the lattice: Otilde refused at a bucket of
2, Otilde+ at 4, and the ambiguity count moves between 24 and 104.

That supersedes reading the ambiguity as a fixed 24-of-444 structure.

No object introduced: the divided class and its equivariance are the library's,
the orbit-coset correspondence is Dutour Sikiric--Hulek Lemma 3.6 (printed p. 22),
and the counts are logs'.
