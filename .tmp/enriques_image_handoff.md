## The published maintained answer

`PredicateSubgroups.ParentMethods.discriminant_image()` (corrected at `4a73ccd`), also
reachable as `group.image_under(lattice.discriminant_representation())`, answers
ρ(Γ) as a generated subgroup of `O(A_L)` for a subgroup retained by complete
character data, exactly, by the joint image and projection:

    ρ(Γ) = π₁( Q ∩ S ),   Q = χ(O(L)),   S = (∩ emb₁(Hᵢ)) ∩ (∩ ker πᵢ)

(for χ = (ρ, det, sn) the joint character, π₁ the projection to the discriminant
factor, the meet taken in the direct product and capped with Q).  Exact under
completeness of the retained character data alone (`χ(χ⁻¹(S)) = Q ∩ S`); no
surjectivity of ρ and no containment of a target in ρ(O(L)) is assumed
(the earlier separate-marginal formula, published at `fd5ab11`, was refuted on
`[[6,0],[0,6]]` and replaced; sharp exactness hypotheses for it are in TRAPS).
No generating family of Γ itself is realized or required, and no element of the
finite image (≈ 4.7·10¹³ for the Enriques coinvariant lattice) is listed.

`subgroup_generator_lifts()` (at `4b8eb75`) furnishes one live, checked isometry
of Γ per selected generator of that image; the subgroup generated by the lifts'
discriminant morphisms is asserted to equal the retained image *as a subgroup*
(mutual generator membership + equal order — never cardinality alone), inside
the same `O(A_L)` object (`coinvariant_form.O()` and the port's `target` are
one cached object).  Each lift's `discriminant_morphism()` is a constructible,
typed element of `O(A_L)`, so a consumer can rebuild
`target.subgroup_on(images)` and assert subgroup equality — the migration
criterion the port owner stated in `.tmp/contract-discriminant-image-identity.md`
at `c68f101e`.  `split_orbit(marked, subgroup)` runs GAP's double-coset machinery
on the generated image, every returned lift checked against the character map.

## What the measurements actually establish about the original generator path

**Retraction, 2026-11-03.** An earlier version of this note stated the original
per-generator line could not terminate "under any correct library answer".
That impossibility inference is retracted: capped experience on one selected
algorithm proves nothing about a different finite generating algorithm.  What
the measurements show is that the *selected* Schreier realization of the
character intersection — the true coset-traversal family, of size
`2·|gens(O(N))|·[O⁺(N):Γ_h]` (≈ 2·10⁷ words at case_3's index 505 920) — did
not terminate within the unchanged 600 s / 6144 MiB guards, twice
(`38523e1`-era and `fd5ab11`-era staged logs; stage-only attribution, no
inner stack in the second run).  A resourced cap on that *selected* family is
not a theorem about other algorithms.

What the evidence does support:

- the selected resolution currently fixed at construction is that Schreier
  family; any answer that materializes exactly it is resource-bounded by its
  size, like any word-family of that cardinality;
- a small presented family of `Γ_h` = lifts of generators of the retained
  discriminant image (constructible today, checked, `4b8eb75`) **union**
  generators of `ker(O⁺(N) → O(A_N)) = ṼO⁺(N)`, whose generation is an open
  lattice-engine capability (`_MISSING_ARITHMETIC_GENERATING_SET`); a primary
  candidate route is on record (Scattone 3.7.3 / Eichler criterion as quoted
  by Dutour Sikirić–Hulek, TeX:1429–1460) but its **applicability to
  `N = U ⊕ U(2) ⊕ E8(-2)` and generation equality with the stable kernel is
  an unmeasured, unproved hypothesis** — `U(2)` is not an unscaled second
  hyperbolic plane, and the twist descent must be established (actual
  embeddings, indices, induced subgroup, transport), not cited.  A focused
  mathematical analysis of exactly this gap is queued as a referee question;
- equivalent downstream assertions fed by the original line: the retained
  discriminant image and its order are computable exactly and without the
  family; orbit splitting is computable through the generated character model.

Nothing here asserts, and nothing measured disproves, that a maintained
finite generating algorithm for the original lines can run inside the guards.
The original per-generator acceptance is open.
