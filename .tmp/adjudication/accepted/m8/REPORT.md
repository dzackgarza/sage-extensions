## 1.  H1 — the embedding is index 2 (PROVED)

**Statement [P].**  `N = U₁ ⊕ ⟨e2,2f2⟩ ⊕ E8(−2)` is an even sublattice of
`L = U₁ ⊕ ⟨e2,f2⟩ ⊕ E8(−2)` of index exactly 2, and `N ≅ U ⊕ U(2) ⊕ E8(−2)`.

*Proof.*  `⟨e2,2f2⟩` has Gram `[[0,2],[2,0]] = U(2)`; the change-of-basis matrix
has determinant 2.  The index is forced: for equal ranks
`[L:N]² = |A_N|/|A_L| = 2¹⁰/2⁸ = 4`, so `[L:N] = 2`.  ∎

The "scaling" of the problem corresponds to the equivalent description
`N ≅ U₁ ⊕ ⟨2e2, f2⟩ ⊕ E8(−2)`, `⟨2e2,f2⟩ ⊂ ⟨e2,f2⟩` still of index 2 (both
`⟨e2,2f2⟩` and `⟨2e2,f2⟩` are ≅ `U(2)`); the form of `N` is *not* the form of `L`
restricted to a primitive sublattice, which is the whole point.

**Discriminant/dual maps [P].**  `L^∨ ⊂ N^∨`, `N ⊂ L ⊂ L^∨ ⊂ N^∨`; computed:

* `A_N = ⟨[f2]⟩ ⊕ ⟨[e2/2]⟩ ⊕ (Z/2)⁸`, order `4·256 = 1024`  (`e2/2 ∈ N^∨`,
  `e2/2 ∉ N`, `f2 ∈ N^∨`, `f2 ∉ N`, `2f2 ∈ N`);
* the natural map `A_L = L^∨/L → N^∨/N = A_N` has kernel `(L^∨ ∩ N)/L = N/L ≅ Z/2`
  (generated by `2f2 + L`) and image of order `2⁷`, hence `[A_N : im A_L] = 8`.

So two "new" 2-torsion directions `[f2]`, `[e2/2]` and one further direction
inside the `E8` part appear when passing to `N`.  These three directions are
exactly where the transport of the hypothesis "acts trivially on the
discriminant" fails (§3).

## 2.  Two lemmas that fix the meaning of the quotation (PROVED)

### Theorem B (isotropic transvections) [P]

For an even lattice `M`, isotropic `e ∈ M` and `x ∈ e^⊥ ∩ M` put

```
E_{e,x}(z) = z + (z,e)·x − (z,x)·e − (z,e)(x,x)/2 · e .
```

Then `E_{e,x} ∈ O(M)`, `det E_{e,x} = 1`, `E_{e,x}` acts trivially on `A_M`, and
`E_{e,x}E_{e,y} = E_{e,x+y} = E_{e,y}E_{e,x}`.

*Why this is the right formula.*  It is pinned by the two properties the quoted
text asserts about Scattone's `E_{e,x}`: the composition law
`E_{e,x}E_{e,y} = E_{e,x+y}` (`Enriques_compu_rev.tex:1446`) and the triviality on
the discriminant (`:1457`).  Both are verified for the formula above in `S2`, on
`L`, and the triviality also on `N` for `e = e2, f1, e1, c1` (i.e. for `e` in the
`U(2)` summand).

*Proof of triviality.*  For `z ∈ M^∨`, `(z,e),(z,x) ∈ Z` since `e,x ∈ M`;
`(x,x) ∈ 2Z` since `M` is even; hence `E_{e,x}(z) − z ∈ M`.  ∎

Consequence **[P]**: the `U(2)` summand is not an obstruction.  `E_{e2,x}` and
`E_{2f2,x}` are isometries of `N` acting trivially on `A_N` for every
`x ∈ e^⊥ ∩ N`.  Note that the problem's phrase `(e_i, N^∨) = ½Z` cannot occur for
`e_i ∈ N`: `(N, N^∨) ⊆ Z` always.  What *is* true is `(e2, N) = 2Z`, i.e.
`div(e2) = 2` — and that has a different, decisive consequence (§6).

### Theorem C (transvection criterion) [P]

For `M` even and `v ∈ M` with `(v,v) = 2a ≠ 0`:
`σ_v ∈ ker(O(M) → O(A_M))  ⟺  v ∈ aM`.

*Proof.*  `σ_v(z) − z = −(z,v)/a · v` is in `M` for all `z ∈ M^∨` iff `v/a ∈ M^∨∨ = M`.  ∎

Consequences **[P]**: `σ_v ∈ ker` for every `(v,v) = ±2`; for `(v,v) = ±4` one
needs `v ∈ 2M`, which is impossible in an even lattice (`(2w,2w) = 4(w,w) ∈ 8Z`),
so **in an even lattice there are no kernel transvections of norm ±4**.  Verified
in `S5` on four norm classes of `N`.

## 3.  H2 — what the quotation supports, and where the transport breaks (REFUTED transport)

### 3a.  The source reading [C]

At `Enriques_compu_rev.tex:1463–1468`:

> "Suppose `L'` and `L` are two integral lattices of rank `n` with `L' ⊂ L` … We can
> compute the stabilizer `S` of `L'` under `Ap(L)` by **Alg 1** and this gets us an
> approximate subgroup `Ap(L')`.  By using **Alg 3** we compute the right coset
> decomposition of `Ap(L)` under `S` …"

So the descent is **the stabiliser**: `Ap(N) = Stab_{Ap(L)}(N)`, generated by the
surviving generators.  It is *not* a restriction of all of `Ap(L)` (elements not
preserving `N` have no action on `N`) and not a conjugate.  The triviality
assertion `:1457` ("The group provided by the Eichler algorithm acts trivially on
the discriminant") is made about the ambient discriminant `A_L` only; it is not a
statement about `N`, and the source nowhere claims that the descended subgroup
generates `ker(O⁺(N) → O(A_N))`.

### 3b.  The hypothesis "U+U+L, L even" fails for N [P]

`Enriques_compu_rev.tex:1429` states the Lemma for `U + U + L` with `L` even; the
generator count `4 + 4(n−1)` (`:1450`) is computed for that shape.  `N` is not of
that shape: if `N ≅ U ⊕ U ⊕ L′` then `L′` has rank 8 and `A_{L′} ≅ A_N = (Z/2)¹⁰`,
but a rank-8 lattice has a discriminant group needing at most 8 generators.
Equivalently, in the language of the criterion itself
(`Gritsenko–Hulek–Sankaran, arXiv:0810.1614`, §"The group E(L) of unimodular
transvections"):

> "One can complete an isotropic element `e ∈ L` to an integral isotropic plane
> `U = Ze ⊕ Zf ⊂ L` if and only if `div(e) = 1`.  We call such an isotropic vector
> *unimodular*."

Computed **[P]**: in `N`, `div(e1) = div(f1) = 1` but `div(e2) = div(2f2) = 2`;
in `L`, `div(f2) = 1`.  So `N` has exactly one unimodular hyperbolic plane, and
the second isotropic direction of `N` cannot be completed.  [C] with the
hypothesis checked.

### 3c.  The transport of triviality fails: 8 explicit generators [P]

The quoted generator list is `4` block generators of `SL₂(Z)²` on the
determinant model `M₂,₂(Z)` (`:1437–1440`) plus the `4(n−1) = 44` transvections
`E_{e_i,v}` with `e_i ∈ {e1,f1,e2,f2}` and `v` running over a `Z`-basis of
`e_i^⊥ ∩ L` (`:1442–1451`) — the "44 candidate transvections".  Since
`M₂,₂(Z) ≅ U ⊕ U` is unimodular on that block, all 48 act trivially on `A_L`
(verified in `S2`, `S6`).

Transported to `N` by the only descent the text supports (the stabiliser):

```
44 quoted transvections:
  32  preserve N and act trivially on A_N      (in Stab_E(N) ∩ ker)
   8  preserve N and act NON-trivially on A_N  (E_{f2,c_j}, j = 1..8)
   4  do not preserve N at all                 (E_{e1,f2}, E_{f1,f2}, E_{f2,e1}, E_{f2,f1})
```

Explicit witness, hand-checkable: `g = E_{f2,c1} ∈ E` is an isometry of `L`, acts
trivially on `A_L`, preserves `N`, and

```
g(e2/2) = e2/2 + f2 + c1/2 ,   g(e2/2) − e2/2 = f2 + c1/2 ∉ N ,
```

so `g` moves the discriminant class `[e2/2] ∈ A_N`.  Hence

> **`Stab_E(N) ⊄ ker(O(N) → O(A_N))`** — the descended group is not even a
> subgroup of the stable kernel.

Verdict H2: the *reading* is proved (stabiliser, Alg 1); the *consequence the
route needs* is refuted.  The 8 elements are exactly the ones whose isotropic
direction `f2` is unimodular in `L` but leaves `N`.

## 4.  H3 — the equality fails (REFUTED)

Two independent reasons.

**(i) The subgroup the descent produces is not inside the kernel** — §3c.

**(ii) Even the "ideal" version of the route has no theorem behind it** [P]+[C].
The generation statements of this circle all need a large 2-rank.  Verbatim from
`arXiv:0810.1614` (Proposition `generators`):

> "Let `L = U ⊕ U₁ ⊕ L₀` be an even lattice with two hyperbolic planes, such that
> `rank₃(L) ≥ 5` and `rank₂(L) ≥ 6`.  Then `ÕSO⁺(L) = O′(L) = E(L) = E_U(L₁)` and
> `ÕO⁺(L) = ⟨Γ^J(L₀), σ₁⟩`."

and Kneser's theorem as they state it (Theorem `thKn`, = Kneser, Satz 4):

> "Let `L` be an even integral lattice of Witt index ≥ 2 over `R`.  We assume that
> `L` represents −2 and that `rank₃(L) ≥ 5` and `rank₂(L) ≥ 6`.  Then `O′(L)` is
> generated by the products of reflections `σ_aσ_b` where `a,b ∈ L` and
> `a² = b² = −2`."

with `rank_p(L)` defined there as "the maximal rank of the sublattices `M` in `L`
such that `det(M)` is coprime to `p`".

Computed **[P]** (`S9`, rank of the Gram matrix mod `p` — equivalent to the
definition, since `det M` odd ⇔ `M/2M` is non-degenerate for the mod-2 form):

| lattice | `rank₂` | `rank₃` | Kneser conditions |
|---|---|---|---|
| `N = U ⊕ U(2) ⊕ E8(−2)` | **2** | 12 | **NO** |
| "ambient" `L = U ⊕ U ⊕ E8(−2)` | **4** | 12 | **NO** |
| `L′ = U ⊕ U(2) ⊕ E8(−1)` | 10 | 12 | **YES** |

`rank₂(N) = 2` is not an accident of my model: GHS state it for this very lattice
(proof of Proposition `enriquessimplyconnected`):

> "The moduli space `E` is associated with the lattice `L = U(2) ⊕ U ⊕ E_8(−2)`,
> which has 2-rank 2 and therefore does not satisfy the Kneser conditions.  But …
> `L′ = U ⊕ U(2) ⊕ E_8(−1)`, since `L` is obtained from `L′` as the sublattice of
> `L′(2)` of index 4 where the generators `e,f` of `U(4)` are replaced by
> `e/2,f/2` … Since `L′` does satisfy the Kneser conditions …"

I verified the transport datum **[P]**: `det L′(2) = 2¹⁴`, `det N = 2¹⁰`, so
`[L′(2) : N] = 4`.  GHS use this identification of *moduli varieties*, not a
group isomorphism: no generator transport `ÕO⁺(L′) → ÕO⁺(N)` is asserted there.

**(iii) What is *not* an obstruction [P].**  Containment is fine: for every
lattice `M` with `N ⊆ M ⊆ N^∨` one has `ker(O(N)→O(A_N)) ⊆ O(M)`.  Proof: `M/N`
is generated by classes in `A_N = N^∨/N`, on which the kernel acts trivially;
verified on `L` and on all 44 intrinsic transvections of `N` (`S4`).  So the
index-2 relation does not by itself prevent an ambient construction — the failure
is caused by the small 2-rank and by the 8 crossing generators.

**Also [P]:** `Γ(2) = ker(O(N)→O(N/2N)) ⊊ ker(O(N)→O(A_N))`; e.g. `σ_{e1+f1}`
lies in the kernel (Theorem C) but moves `N/2N` because `(f1, e1+f1) = 1` is odd.
So the object in question is the stable kernel, not a level-2 congruence subgroup,
and any generator statement must produce elements outside `Γ(2)`.

Verdict H3: **refuted**.  `ker(O⁺(N)→O(A_N))` is not generated by anything the
descent from `U ⊕ U ⊕ E8(−2)` can produce, and the cited generation theorems fail
their hypotheses on both `N` and `L`.

## 5.  H4 — spinor norms (COMPUTED, PROVED)

Convention taken from the primary source (`arXiv:0810.1614`, §1):

> `sn_K(g) = (−(v₁,v₁)/2) ⋯ (−(v_m,m)/2) (K^×)²` for `g = σ_{v₁}…σ_{v_m}`, and
> `O⁺(L) = O(L) ∩ ker sn_R`, `ÕO⁺(L) = ÕO(L) ∩ O⁺(L)`, `O′(L) = SO(L) ∩ ker sn_Q`.

So `sn(σ_v) = −(v,v)/2 · Q*²`: a reflection lies in `O⁺` **iff its norm is
negative** (this is the signature-(2,n) convention, chosen in GHS precisely so that
reflections in negative vectors preserve the domain component).

Every named generator was factorised into reflections with exact arithmetic and
the spinor norm computed from the factorisation (`S6`):

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| `E_{e2,c1} ∈ E` on `L` | 1 | 2 | `144 = 12²` | yes | **yes** |
| `E_{f2,c1}` (descent witness) | 1 | 2 | `196 = 14²` | yes | **yes** |

Hence:

* every isotropic transvection `E_{e,x}` **is** in `O′ = ÕSO⁺` (and a fortiori in

  outside `O⁺`.

Verdict H4: `O⁺` is *not* where the route fails; the correction the route needs
lies in the discriminant action (§3c), not in the spinor selection.

## 6.  The sharpest statement that survives

**No published theorem generates `ker(O⁺(N)→O(A_N))` on this `N`.**  GHS say
explicitly that this lattice fails the Kneser conditions, and my computation shows
the same for the ambient `U ⊕ U ⊕ E8(−2)` (`rank₂ = 4 < 6`).

What can be maintained, with exact hypotheses and counts:

1. **[P] (inclusions on `N`).**  `ÕO⁺(N) = ker(O(N)→O(A_N)) ∩ O⁺(N)` contains
   * all `σ_v` with `(v,v) = −2` (roots of `N`; Theorem C and the GHS convention);
   * every isotropic transvection `E_{e,x}` with `e, x ∈ N`, `e^⊥x` (Theorem B);
   * in particular the whole unimodular transvection group
     `E(N) = ⟨t(e,a), t(f,a) : a ∈ U(2) ⊕ E8(−2)⟩`, generated by **2·10 = 20**
     transvections (the analogue of the quoted `4 + 4(n−1)` count; GHS state
     `E(L) ⊆ ÕSO⁺(L)` in general);
   * `ker(O(N)→O(A_N)) ⊆ O(M)` for every `N ⊆ M ⊆ N^∨` (so ambient lattices cause
     no containment problem);
   * on the `E8` side the kernel is small: `O(E8(−2)) = O(E8) = W(E8)` (since
     `−1 = w₀ ∈ W(E8)`), `|W(E8)| = 696729600`, and `|O(A_{E8(−2)})| = 348364800 =
     |W(E8)|/2` (computed); `±1` acts trivially on `E8/2E8`, so `Õ(E8(−2))` has
     order at least 2 and the image in `O(A_{E8(−2)})` at most `|W(E8)|/2`.  That
     the image is all of `O(A_{E8(−2)})`, i.e. `Õ(E8(−2)) = {±1}`, is the standard
     fact and is consistent with the three `W(E8)`-orbits `1, 120, 135` quoted at
     `:1460–1461`; I did **not** verify surjectivity computationally, so this last
     equality is **[U]**.  Either way the `E8` isometries the paper *adds* at
     `:1458–1461` are overwhelmingly **not** in the kernel.
2. **[C], hypotheses verified (the Kneser-satisfying model).**  For
   `L′ = U ⊕ U(2) ⊕ E8(−1)`: even, signature (2,10), represents −2, `rank₂ = 10`,
   `rank₃ = 12` — so all Kneser conditions hold (**[P]** for the ranks) and
   Kneser's Satz 4 as quoted by GHS gives
   `O′(L′) = ÕSO⁺(L′) = ⟨σ_aσ_b : a² = b² = −2⟩`; with GHS's Corollary
   `comparespinors` (`O′(L) = ÕSO⁺(L)`) and the relation
   `ÕO⁺(L) = ⟨ÕSO⁺(L), σ_{e−f}⟩` used in their §"Strong Approximation" preamble
   (valid whenever `L` contains a hyperbolic plane, as `L′` does), the stable
   kernel of `L′` is generated by the `(−2)`-reflections together with one
   reflection.  Expected generator count: one `σ_a` per
   `ÕO⁺`-orbit of `(−2)`-vectors; by the proof of GHS's Theorem `com1` every class
   modulo the commutator is a product of reflections in *distinct* orbits, so the
   orbit representatives give a generating set.  The number of such orbits is not
   computed here.
3. **[U] not available.**  There is no statement in the accessible primary sources
   that transports (2) to `N`; GHS's `L ⊂ L′(2)` (index 4, verified) is used only
   to identify moduli varieties.  Whether `ÕO⁺(N)` is generated by
   `{σ_a : a² = −2} ∪ E(N)` (plus finitely many orbit representatives) is **open**
   on the evidence available here, and `rank₂(N) = 2` means the standard proofs do
   not apply.

Also **[U]**: Scattone's Memoir (*Mem. AMS* 70-2, no. 374, 1987; bibliography
entry at `Enriques_compu_rev.tex:1938–1939`) is not reachable from this VM, so
Proposition 3.7.3 and the definition of `E_{e,x}` could not be read in the original.
The formula used for `E_{e,x}` is the unique one compatible with both quoted
