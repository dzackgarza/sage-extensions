And no finer key of the same kind exists: the key is the rho(Gamma)-orbit of the
line's divided class pair, whose fibres are the double cosets A \ F / F_P of
Dutour Sikiric--Hulek, Lemma 3.6, printed p. 22; the fibre over a class pair is a
torsor under Stab_F(c_0)/F_P, so any invariant that is a function of the line's
discriminant class is constant on it.  A finer invariant must read something
that is not a function of the class -- the stabilizer's own image, or an actual
isometry.

So the two kernel rows of T_Co are blocked on the invariant, not on cost or on
the host.  The options are a design change that carries the stabilizer's image,
or accepting the refusal as this lattice's answer.
