fix: a cusp row is admitted as its orbits, not by comparing them

Both rows over cusps ended in the literal ingress, which identifies repeated
points through a table of them; a cusp's hash is constant over its universe --
two cusps of one orbit need not share a span, so a cusp has no cheap canonical
key -- so every cusp of the row was compared with every other, and each
comparison is a transporter question through the divided-class invariant.  The
arithmetic subgroup's row and the full orthogonal group's row are the two sites,
the second over its own cusp engine, which has the same constant hash and whose
equality is a port transporter search.

The linear form is the one `tits_building_incidence` already used three hundred
lines below: supply `from_indexed`'s inverse and skip the dedup, which also skips
the second table the construction builds for itself.  It is named once, at the
owner of the ingress, as `_distinct_finite_ordered_set`, whose docstring states
the caller's obligation -- two equal points leave the enumeration
non-injective.  The cusps' own equality still answers for a candidate that is
not one of the retained objects, so membership and ranking off the image are
what they were; that question is asked when it is put, not once per pair while
the row is built.

Measured on `Otilde(T_Co)` (`T_Co = <2> + U(2) + E8(-2)`, adopted runtime, port
`991b88b0`):

- 100 plane cusps: the old ingress 249.986 s, the repaired admission under a
  millisecond.  Element by element the two rows are one row: 100 of 100 points
  retained in their own positions, no position whose span differs, the inverse
  `0..99` on both, a cusp rebuilt from a member's span placed at 0 by both and a
  cusp of the subgroup's other rank by neither, and 100 distinct spans among the
  100 cusps -- so the dedup never dropped a point.
- 1 000 / 10 000 / 100 000 planes: 1.77 / 2.11 / 2.11 us a plane at admission,
  against a per-plane build of 6.86 / 6.47 / 6.79 ms (first band against last
  7.71 -> 6.25, 6.78 -> 6.28, 6.37 -> 6.25) and a row built in 206.3 / 277.5 /
  886.2 s.  Flat to 5% over a factor of 100 in the row's length.  At the row's
  own 189720 planes that is 1288 s of planes, a 207 s transversal and 0.40 s of
  admission, 25 minutes, where the ingress alone was 14.4 years a pass.
- 527 line cusps of the same subgroup: 245.2 s of walk, and the old ingress's
  11.1-12.0 ms a pair was about 1600 s on top of it.

The row's contents do not change: the walk yields one representative per orbit,
so the table could only ever answer "not equal", and the new test asserts that
pairwise distinctness as the obligation the admission now carries.  A red test
for the timing is not possible inside the call ceiling and is argued, with these
measurements, in `results/issue598-the-cusp-rows-ingress-is-linear.md`.
